What we do

Atiras makes STEM kits

Atiras builds STEM kits that turn curiosity into real, working projects. Our kits have been on stall tables and in classrooms, built for young makers who want to make things with their own hands.

Idea to Kit is our newest way in. You bring the idea, we map out the parts and the first steps, and you can receive a kit to start building.

How it works

Three steps from idea to build

  1. Share your idea. Tell us what you want to build and why, in your own words.
  2. Get a starting path. A plain list of the first steps and the components you would need.
  3. Receive a kit. Parts to start building your project.

What to expect

Good to know

What the form asks

Your name, age, location, who is applying, project name and purpose, what you want to build, its current stage, the help you need, any constraints, a target date or budget, and how to contact you. Shipping details are optional now and can be provided later if delivery is needed.
